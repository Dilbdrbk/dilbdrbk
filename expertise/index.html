This approach follows Koray Tugberk Gubur's advanced topical authority methodology, which I studied and am certified in.

13 Glossary

The terms I use when I plan and explain SEO work.

The words behind the work

Central entity
The main subject a website is about. Every page should connect back to it, so search engines can tell what the site is an authority on.
Source context
Why the website exists and how it earns. It decides which attributes of the central entity matter most and how deep each one should go.
Attribute
A property of an entity, such as its types, cost, problems, location or brands. Attributes become the sections and pages of a topical map.
Topical map
A planned structure of every topic and subtopic a site should cover, which of them deserve their own page, and how the pages link to each other.
Topical authority
The trust a search engine gives a site on a topic after the site covers that topic in depth, accurately and consistently over time.
Core and outer section
The core section holds the topics closest to how the business earns. The outer section holds related topics that build relevance and link back to the core.
Macro and micro context
The macro context is the one main topic of a page. Micro contexts are the supporting subtopics inside it. A page should keep a single macro context.
Contextual coverage
How completely a page or site answers the questions that belong to its topic, including the questions people ask next.
Query network
The group of related searches around one need: the main query, its variations, and the questions people ask before and after it.
Cost of retrieval
The effort a search engine spends to crawl, understand and rank a page. Clean structure, clear answers and less wasted content lower it.
Query fan-out
How AI search splits one question into several smaller searches, reads the results, and combines them into a single answer.
Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)
Optimizing content and entities so a brand is used and cited in AI-generated answers on ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews.
Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)
Structuring content so a passage can be lifted as a direct answer in featured snippets, voice results or AI answers.
Exact match domain (EMD)
A domain name that matches a target search query word for word. How much it still helps is one of the variables I test.
Google stacking
Building a set of interlinked Google properties, such as Sites, Docs, Sheets and Drive files, around a business to support its brand and local signals.
