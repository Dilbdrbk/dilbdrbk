Expertise

SEO expertise, from entities to AI answers

Semantic SEO is my specialty. It works because it sits on top of the fundamentals: technical health, strong pages, local signals and links. These are the twelve areas I work in.

01 Core

Semantic & entity SEO

I take a holistic approach to semantic SEO. The goal is to establish a website as a leading authority within its niche: define the central entity, connect its attributes and related entities, and give every page one clear topic and intent.

This approach follows Koray Tugberk Gubur's advanced topical authority methodology, which I studied and am certified in.

02 Core

Topical maps & content strategy

I build topical maps and content architectures that cover a niche in depth, which strengthens a site's authority and relevance. Content strategy pairs entity-based optimization with well-structured briefs, so each page satisfies both search intent and semantic relevance.

03 Foundation

Technical SEO

Technical SEO is part of every strategy I run, from crawl and indexing issues to site and information architecture. At RankMeTop I have executed technical fixes as an SEO executive and built technical SEO strategies as a specialist to grow organic traffic.

04 Foundation

On-page SEO

On-page work covers titles, headings, copy, internal links and page structure. I have optimized e-commerce category and product pages and the information architecture around them, so the right page answers the right query.

05 Foundation

Local SEO

For service businesses I manage Google Business Profiles, build citations and set local SEO strategy. Local niches I have worked in include plumbing, HVAC, flooring, fencing, pest control and home improvement.

06 Foundation

Off-page SEO & link building

My SEO career started with Google stacking, off-page link building and citation building for local campaigns. Today off-page work supports the topical authority built on the site itself.

07 Foundation

Keyword research

Keyword research comes before every map and every page: find the queries, group them by intent, and assign each group to the page that should rank for it.

08 Scale

Programmatic SEO

I design and deploy programmatic SEO systems that scale content across large keyword sets.

09 AI search

GEO, AEO & AI search optimization

Search now happens inside AI answers too. I optimize for Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) and Answer Engine Optimization (AEO), structuring content and entities so brands surface within AI-generated answers on ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews.

10 AI search

Reddit SEO

Reddit has become a strong ranking and reference source in both Google and AI search. I use strategic community participation, discussion-driven content and brand mentions to build topical relevance, referral traffic and visibility in traditional and AI-generated results.

11 Research

SEO patents & whitepaper research

I research Google's SEO-related patents and whitepapers to identify emerging ranking signals, algorithmic priorities and advances in semantic search. Then I translate complex search and NLP findings into SEO strategy that fits how Google's systems are changing.

12 Research

SEO hypothesis testing

Much of my SEO knowledge comes from testing. I regularly run experiments on AI-generated content, exact match domains (EMDs) and technical variables to validate what actually moves rankings, and refine my strategies from the results.
