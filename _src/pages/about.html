About Dil Bahadur B.K.

I'm a result-driven semantic SEO specialist from Kathmandu, Nepal. My focus is semantic SEO, built on a foundation of traditional, local, on-page and off-page SEO.

01 What I care about

Search that pays for itself

I'm passionate about data-driven strategy and quality content. The aim of every project is the same: grow organic traffic, generate leads, lift rankings and give the business a real return on what it spends on SEO.

I set SEO strategy across local service and e-commerce projects and work with developers and content teams to get changes implemented. I own projects end to end, from strategy through execution and performance analysis.

02 How I got here

From link building to semantic SEO

My SEO career started in December 2023 with data entry, Google stacking and link building for local SEO campaigns. From there I moved to SEO intern, learning the fundamentals on real projects, then to SEO executive, where I handled keyword research, on-page work, technical fixes and Google Business Profiles.

As an SEO specialist from November 2024, I built technical, on-page and local SEO strategies, optimized e-commerce category and product pages, and grew topical authority through content strategy. Today I work as a semantic SEO specialist.

Before SEO, I earned a Diploma in Information Technology from Nepal Banepa Polytechnic Institute and trained in the React and Django frameworks at Broadway Infosys. That technical base still helps when I work with developers.

03 How I keep learning

Methodology, patents and tests

I follow Koray Tugberk Gubur's advanced topical authority methodology and hold his Topical Authority certification. It shapes how I think about entities, topical maps and semantic relevance.

I also read Google's SEO-related patents and whitepapers to understand ranking signals, algorithmic priorities and how semantic search is changing. Then I test. Much of what I know comes from experiments on AI-generated content, exact match domains and technical variables.

04 Principles

The ideas behind every strategy I write.

See them as diagrams

Principles I work by

  • 01

    Search reads entities, not just words

    I structure a site around its central entity and the attributes people search for, so its meaning is easy to understand.

  • 02

    Every page has to earn its place

    A topic gets its own page only when it has its own intent and enough substance. Otherwise it becomes a section.

  • 03

    Answer first

    The first lines of a page or section answer its question. Background and detail come after.

  • 04

    Write so machines can quote you

    Plain facts, clear lists and structured data make a passage easy to lift into a snippet or an AI answer.

  • 05

    Go to the source

    Google's patents and whitepapers explain the signals behind rankings better than most summaries of them.

  • 06

    Test before you trust

    An SEO idea is a hypothesis until a controlled test shows it moves rankings.
