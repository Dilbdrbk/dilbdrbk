01 Method

How are the Missed in SEO videos made?

Each episode starts from one source page, and its script is checked against that page on the day the video is built, because documentation changes. The source is named at the end of every video and under every player on this page.

The voice-over is an AI text-to-speech voice, and the on-screen captions follow it word for word. I approve every script before the video goes live, and the guides linked under each video cover the topic in full, as the editorial policy describes.

The videos play from this site, so no YouTube player or third-party tracking loads on this page.
