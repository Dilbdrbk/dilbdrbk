Semantic content checker

Paste a draft with its headings marked as # H1, ## H2 and ### H3, and enter the main topic phrase. The checker tests the draft against the measurable rules from the checklist above: answer-first headings, the topic phrase under every main section, paragraph length, concrete examples after plural nouns, heading order, dashes and overused filler words. It checks the writing, not the facts, so it is not a Google score.

Results appear here, with the line of each issue.

The filler-word list and the 2 to 4 sentence paragraph rule follow the writing rules every guide on this site passes before publishing. Nothing you paste leaves your browser.
